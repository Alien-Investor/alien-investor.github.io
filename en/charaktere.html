The Crew

Alien Investor — alien-investor.org

"One alien doesn't make a channel. You need someone watching the numbers, someone asleep, and someone delivering the facts."

Every video runs through the same cockpit: an alien with an opinion, an onboard AI with principles, a cat nothing impresses, and a voice that never gets a face. Four roles, clearly split — here's who they are.

Host

Alien Investor

Extraterrestrial, observer, rebel with a message. Green skin, black eyes, two antennae — and a suit with a red tie, because rebellious content doesn't need a chaotic look. Only appears in the intro and outro, stays out of the way otherwise. No guru, no doom-caller — just an opinion from outside.

"Trust no one — manage your finances yourself."

Onboard AI

Kerstin

Cyan-blue hologram, bob haircut, watches prices and metrics from the cockpit. Level-headed, loyal, drily funny — a sidekick, not a co-host.

One detail gives away where she's from: Kerstin is a language model trained on German. Her English is genuinely bad — she'd rather skip a technical term entirely than get it wrong. On this channel, that's not a bug. That's character.

Ship's Cat

Miu

Grey-black tabby, white muzzle, green eyes. Sleeps through markets crashing or exploding outside — Miu has no opinion on it, and that's the whole point. Never speaks, never comments. The calm counterweight to every bit of market drama.
