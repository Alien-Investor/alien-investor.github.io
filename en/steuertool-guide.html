BTC Tax Tool – The Guide

From CSV export to tax office report: the video and the six steps at a glance

"The entire calculation runs on your device. No upload, no account, no tracking. In the browser or as an app for Linux and Android."

Note: The interface is available in English: in the web version switch with the EN button at the top right (or open the tool with ?lang=en), in the apps with the language button at the top left. The reports themselves stay in German, because they are documents for the German tax office (§ 23 EStG). The video is in German with English subtitles.

The 6 steps at a glance

  1. What you need. The CSV exports of your brokers and wallets, complete from your first purchase. The tool reads BitBox, Sparrow, Electrum, Trezor Suite, Ledger Wallet, 21bitcoin, Bison, Swissquote, Strike, Pocket and Bisq, other exchanges via an export from CoinTracking or Blockpit. Purchases without an export (cash, P2P) go into a manual_buys.csv.
  2. Load your files. Drag all files into the dashed zone at once or select them; a ZIP works too. The order does not matter; the app detects every broker from the file contents.
  3. Check detection. The table shows each file with its detected type. Entries marked orange count as noKYC or may contain noKYC purchases. Switch a dedicated noKYC wallet to "… (noKYC)" in the dropdown; a wallet export cannot reveal that. An unrecognized file locks the calculation until you pick its type or ignore it.
  4. Compute the report. One click on "Calculate report". On start the app loads its calculation core, after that it is fast. The system log shows how many transactions were read from each source, and all warnings.
  5. The results. For each tax year the Steuerreport and the formal Steuernachweis (both for the tax office), purchases and sales as CSV, and internal files with a warning sign for your own bookkeeping: the wallet check with the real names of your wallets and, if you have noKYC transactions, "noKYC intern".
  6. Save. One document on its own or all of them as a ZIP. The internal files sit in the ZIP folder INTERN-NICHT-WEITERGEBEN ("internal, do not pass on"). Your brokers' original exports serve as evidence.

All details, including where to find the export in each program, are in the manual behind the "?" at the top right of the app. It is the same in the web version, the Flatpak and the Android app, and in the apps it also works offline.

Frequently asked

Several wallets and accounts?

According to the German Federal Ministry of Finance letter of 06.03.2025 (para. 62), holdings are considered per wallet. The app therefore applies FiFo separately for each wallet. It also keeps every exchange account as a holding of its own; that is a decision of the tool. On a transfer between your wallets, the sending wallet gives up its oldest units; purchase date and cost travel with them. The app matches transfers by transaction ID, otherwise by amount and time (48 hours). An outgoing transfer without a matching incoming one counts as a transfer to one of your wallets that was not loaded and is reported in the log. If it was a payment or a sale, it belongs in manual_sales.csv. In the documents for the tax office your wallets are only named by their kind, such as "BitBox-Wallet" or, with several, "BitBox-Wallet 1", "BitBox-Wallet 2" (likewise "Sparrow-Wallet" etc.). Accounts from CoinTracking or Blockpit appear there with their names. The real names of your wallets appear only in the internal files.

Fees paid in bitcoin?

On a transfer between your wallets the transferred holding stays tax-neutral. The app treats the network or withdrawal fee paid in bitcoin as an exchange for a service and books it as a small disposal of the fee share (derived from the German Federal Ministry of Finance letter of 06.03.2025, paras. 33, 54, 60), valued at the daily closing price (para. 91). The report shows it in the section "GEBÜHREN IN BITCOIN".

Gave bitcoin away or donated it?

For a genuine gift without anything in return, put a word like "gift", "donation", "Spende", "Geschenk" or "Schenkung" into the transaction note before exporting (BitBoxApp; in Sparrow, Electrum and Trezor Suite into the label; not possible with Ledger). The app then books the line as a gratuitous transfer: no sale, no gain, but the coins leave your holdings. The report lists purchase date and cost per lot, because the acquisition is attributed to the recipient (§ 23 (1) sentence 3 EStG).

What about noKYC sales?

The same tax rules as for all sales apply, and their gains belong in your tax return as well. The internal file "noKYC intern" shows the FiFo matching, the holding period and the gains for them. The Steuerreport and the Steuernachweis do not include these gains, not even in the exemption-limit check, although the limit applies to all private disposals of the year together. The separation serves your privacy and clean bookkeeping, not concealment.

Purchases in CHF or USD (Swissquote, Pocket)?

The app converts them to EUR at the ECB reference rate of the purchase day (weekend or holiday: last rate before). The rate table ships with the app; no rate service is queried. If a purchase lies after the table's data date, the app stops with an explanation instead of using an old rate.

My broker is missing?

Enter the purchases in a manual_buys.csv (date,btc_amount,eur_amount,note,kyc,wallet), the sales in a manual_sales.csv (format in the repository, docs/DATENFORMATE.md). For brokers with identity verification, such as Coinbase, set the column kyc to ja; otherwise the purchases count as noKYC. And send the CSV header line without real data to kontakt@alien-investor.org. New parsers arrive with an update.

Something does not work?

"Report a bug" at the bottom of the app. There you can create a diagnosis without file names, amounts, exact dates, addresses or wallet names, and you see all of it before sending. Support covers technical and file-format questions, not tax questions.

Web or app?

Both use the same calculation core, free and open source. The web version loads its code from the server on every visit. The calculation still runs on your device; you trust the server to deliver the published code. The apps bring everything along and have no internet access. Full control comes with the command-line tool from the repository.

What does the tool not calculate?

It only calculates bitcoin; Lightning payments are not covered. It is not meant for mining, staking, lending or commercial trading; a tax advisor can help there.
